How we interview engineers

Our hiring process has four steps and usually takes two to three weeks.

  1. Recruiter call (30 minutes): motivation, logistics, and questions about Acme.
  2. Take-home exercise: a small billing service in TypeScript. We ask you to spend no more than three hours on it.
  3. Technical interview (90 minutes): we extend your take-home together, then a system design round on designing an idempotent payments API.
  4. Values interview with the hiring manager (45 minutes): behavioural questions about ownership, disagreement and written communication.

We do not use whiteboard puzzles or brain-teasers.
